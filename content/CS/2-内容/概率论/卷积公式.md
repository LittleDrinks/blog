$$
\begin{align}
f_{X+Y}(z)&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(z-y,y)\mathrm{d}y \\
&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x,z-x)\mathrm{d}x \\
&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_X(z-y)f_Y(y)\mathrm{d}y \\
&=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_X(x)f_Y(z-x)\mathrm{d}x
\end{align}
$$
记作 $f_{X+Y}=f_X*f_Y$
