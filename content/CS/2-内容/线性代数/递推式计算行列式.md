> [!faq]- 计算行列式 $D_{2n} = \begin{vmatrix}a & 0 & \cdots &0&0&\cdots & 0&b \\0 & a & \cdots &0&0& \cdots&b&0 \\\vdots & \vdots & \ddots &\vdots&\vdots& \ddots&\vdots&\vdots \\0& 0 & \cdots & a & b & \cdots&0&0\\0 & 0&\cdots &c&d & \cdots & 0&0 \\\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots &\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\0 & c&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&d&0\\c & 0 & \cdots & 0&0&\cdots&0&d\\\end{vmatrix}_{2n \times 2n}$
> 按第一行展开可得：
> $$
> a (-1)^{1+1} \begin{vmatrix}0 & \cdots &0&0&\cdots & 0&b \\a & \cdots &0&0& \cdots&b&0 \\ \vdots & \ddots &\vdots&\vdots& \ddots&\vdots&\vdots \\0 & \cdots & a & b & \cdots&0&0\\0&\cdots &c&d & \cdots & 0&0 \\ \vdots & \ddots & \vdots &\vdots&\ddots&\vdots&\vdots\\ c&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&d&0\\0 & \cdots & 0&0&\cdots&0&d\\\end{vmatrix}_{(2n-1) \times (2n-1)}
> + b (-1)^{1+2n} \begin{vmatrix}a & 0 & \cdots &0&0&\cdots & 0 \\0 & a & \cdots &0&0& \cdots&b \\\vdots & \vdots & \ddots &\vdots&\vdots& \ddots&\vdots \\0& 0 & \cdots & a & b & \cdots&0\\0 & 0&\cdots &c&d & \cdots & 0 \\\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots &\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\0 & c&\cdots&0&0&\cdots&d\\c & 0 & \cdots & 0&0&\cdots&0\\\end{vmatrix}_{(2n-1) \times (2n-1)}
> $$
> 都按最后一行展开：
> $$
> D_{2n}=(ad-bc)D_{2(n-1)}
> $$
> 以此作为递推公式有：
> $$
> \begin{align}
> D_{2n} &= (ad - bc) D_{2 (n-1)}\\
> &= (ad - bc)^2 D_{2 (n-2)} = (ad - bc)^3 D_{2 (n-3)} = \cdots = (ad - bc)^{n-1} D_2\\
> &= (ad - bc)^{n-1} \begin{vmatrix}a & b \\c & d\end{vmatrix} = (ad - bc)^n\\
> \end{align}
> $$
