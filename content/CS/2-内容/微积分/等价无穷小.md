**三角相关**
$\sin x\sim\tan x\sim\arcsin x\sim\arctan x\sim x$
$\displaystyle 1-\cos x\sim \frac12 x^2$
$\displaystyle x-\sin x\sim x-\arcsin x\sim\frac16 x^3$
$\displaystyle x-\tan x\sim x-\arctan x\sim \frac13 x^3$
**幂指对**
$(1+x)^a-1\sim ax$
$e^x-1\sim x$，$a^x-1\sim x\ln a$
$\ln(1+x)\sim x$
