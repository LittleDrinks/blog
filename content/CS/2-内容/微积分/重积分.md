积分前先画出积分区域，重积分可以交换积分次序。

> [!faq]- 计算累次积分 $\displaystyle I=\int_0^1dy\int_y^1(\dfrac{e^{x^2}}{x}-e^{y^2})dx$
> $$
> \begin{align}
> I & {\color{red}{\overset{积分换次}{=}}} \int_0^1 dx \int_0^x \left (\frac{e^{x^2}}{x}- e^{y^2}\right) dy = \int_0^1 \left (e^{x^2} - \left (\int_0^x e^{y^2} dy\right)\right) dx\\
> & = \int_0^1 e^{x^2} dx - \int_0^1 \left (\int_0^x e^{y^2} dy\right) dx {\color{red}{\overset{分部积分}{=}}} \int_0^1 e^{x^2} dx - \left.\left (x \int_0^x e^{y^2} dy\right)\right|_0^1 + \int_0^1 e^{x^2} \cdot x dx\\
> & = \int_0^1 e^{x^2} dx - \int_0^1 e^{y^2} dy + \frac{1}{2} e^{x^2} \bigg|_0^1\\
> & = \frac{e - 1}{2}
> \end{align}
> $$ 

三重积分可以采取先一后二，先二后一
