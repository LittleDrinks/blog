# 幂指对函数
$\displaystyle\int x^\mu=\frac{x^{\mu+1}}{\mu+1}+C$
$\displaystyle\int a^xdx=\frac{a^x}{\ln a}+C$
$\displaystyle\int e^xdx=e^x+C$
$\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x}dx=\ln\left|x\right|+C$

# （反）三角函数
$\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{1+x^2}=\arctan x+C$
$\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\arcsin x+C$
$\displaystyle\int \sin xdx=-\cos x+C$
$\displaystyle\int \cos xdx=\sin x+C$
$\displaystyle\int \sec^2xdx=\tan x+C$
$\displaystyle\int \csc^2xdx=\cot x+C$
$\displaystyle\int \tan x\cdot\sec xdx=\sec x+C$
$\displaystyle\int \cot x\cdot\csc xdx=-\csc x+C$
$\displaystyle\int\tan xdx=-\ln|\cos x|+C$
$\displaystyle\int\cot xdx=\ln|\sin x|+C$
$\displaystyle\int\sec x=\ln\left|\sec x+\tan x \right|+C$
$\displaystyle\int\csc x=\ln\left|\csc x-\cot x\right|+C$
> [!note]
> $\displaystyle\int\sec xdx=\int\frac{\cos x}{\cos^2 x}dx=\int\frac{d\sin x}{1-\sin^2 x}=\frac{1}{2}\int[\frac{1}{1+\sin x}+\frac{1}{1-\sin x}]d\sin x=\frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x}\right|+C$
> $\displaystyle\int\sec xdx=\int\frac{\sec x(\sec x+\tan x)}{\sec x+\tan x}dx=\int\frac{d(\sec x+\tan x)}{\sec x+\tan x}=\ln\left|\sec x+\tan x\right|+C$

# 根式
$\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{a^2+x^2}dx=\frac{1}{a}\arctan\frac{x}{a}+C$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{x^2-a^2}dx=\frac{1}{2a}\ln|\frac{x-a}{x+a}|+C$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}dx=\arcsin\frac{x}{a}+C$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}dx=\ln(x+\sqrt{x^2+a^2})+C$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2-a^2}}=\ln|x+\sqrt{x^2-a^2}|+C$

# 定积分的化简

区间再现公式：$\int_a^bf(x)dx=\int_a^bf(a+b-x)dx$
推论：$\displaystyle\int_0^\pi xf(\sin x)dx = \frac{\pi}{2}\int_0^\pi f(\sin x)dx$
点火公式：$\displaystyle\int\sin^n xdx=\begin{cases}\frac{n-1}{n}\cdot\frac{n-3}{n-2}\cdots\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2} & n\text{为偶数}\\\frac{n-1}{n}\cdot\frac{n-3}{n-2}\cdots\frac{1}{1} & n\text{为奇数}\end{cases}$
几何意义：$\displaystyle\int_0^a\sqrt{a^2-x^2}dx=\frac{a^2\pi}{4}$，$\displaystyle\int_0^a\sqrt{2ax-x^2}dx=\frac{a^2\pi}{4}$
奇函数：$\displaystyle\int_{-a}^af(x)dx=0$
偶函数：$\displaystyle\int_{-a}^af(x)dx=2\int_{0}^{a}f(x)dx$
对称性：$\displaystyle\int_{a}^{a+nT}f(x)dx=n\int_{-T/2}^{T/2}f(x)dx$
