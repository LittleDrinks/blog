$\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{4+x}=\int\frac{d(4+x)}{4+x}$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4-x}}=-\int(4-x)^{-\frac12}d(4-x)$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{4+x^2}=\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{d(\frac{x}{2})}{1+(\frac{x}{2})^2}$
$\displaystyle \int\frac{x}{4+x^2}dx=\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{d(4+x^2)}{4+x^2}$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{x^2}{4+x^2}=\int[1-\frac{4}{4+x^2}]dx$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{4-x^2}=\frac{1}{4}\int[\frac{1}{2-x}+\frac{1}{2+x}]dx$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4-x^2}}=\frac12\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-(\frac{x}{2})^2}}$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{3-2x-x^2}}=\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4-(x+1)^2}}$
$\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4x-x^2}}=\int\frac{d(x-2)}{\sqrt{4-(x-2)^2}}$ 或 $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{4x-x^2}}=2\int\frac{d\sqrt x}{\sqrt{4-x}}=\int\frac{d\sqrt x}{\sqrt{1-(\frac{\sqrt{x}}{2})^2}}$
