二维一般形式：$\int_Lds=\int_L\sqrt{dx^2+dy^2}$
直角坐标：$L:y=y(x)(a\leq x\leq b)$，则 $\displaystyle L=\int_a^b\sqrt{1+[y'(x)]^2}dx$
参数方程：$L:\begin{cases}x=x(t)\\y=y(t)\end{cases}(\alpha\leq t\leq \beta)$，则 $L=\displaystyle\int_\alpha^\beta\sqrt{[x'(t)]^2+[y'(t)]^2}dt$
极坐标：$L:r=r(\theta)(\alpha\leq\theta\leq\beta)$，则 $L=\displaystyle\int_\alpha^\beta\sqrt{[r(\theta)]^2+[r'(\theta)]^2}d\theta$
