# 常见微分公式
$\displaystyle\int f(ax+b)dx = \frac{1}{a}\int f(ax+b)d(ax+b)$
$\displaystyle\int f(e^x)e^xdx=\int f(e^x)de^x$
$\displaystyle\int f(\ln x)\frac{1}{x}dx=\int f(\ln x)d\ln x$

> [!example] 
> $\displaystyle\int [\frac{f(x)}{f'(x)}-\frac{f''(x)f^2(x)}{f'^{3}(x)}]dx=\int\frac{f(x)}{f'(x)}[1-\frac{f''(x)f(x)}{f'^2(x)}]dx=\int\frac{f(x)}{f'(x)}\cdot\frac{f'^2(x)-f''(x)f(x)}{f'^2(x)}dx=\int\frac{f(x)}{f'(x)}d(\frac{f(x)}{f'(x)})=\frac{1}{2}[\frac{f(x)}{f'(x)}]^2+C$

> [!example] 
> $\displaystyle\int\frac{x^2+1}{x^4-x^2+1}dx=\int\frac{1+x^{-2}}{x^2-1+x^{-2}}=\int\frac{d(x-x^{-1})}{(x-x^{-1})^2+1}=\arctan(x+x^{-1})+C$

# 万能凑幂法
$\displaystyle\int f(x^n)\frac{1}{x}dx=\frac{1}{n}\int f(x^n)\frac{1}{x^n}dx^n$
$\displaystyle \int f(x^n)x^{n-1}dx = \frac{1}{n}\int f(x^n)dx^n$

> [!example] 
> $\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x(x^{10}+1)}$
> 法一，$=\displaystyle\int\frac{(x^{10}+1)-x^{10}}{x(x^{10}+1)}dx$
> 法二，$=\displaystyle\frac{dx}{x(x^{10}+1)}=\frac{1}{10}\int\frac{dx^{10}}{x^{10}(x^{10}+1)}$
> 法三，$=\displaystyle\int\frac{dx}{x^{11}(1+x^{-10})}=-\frac{1}{10}\int\frac{dx^{-10}}{1+x^{-10}}$

# 三角相关
$\displaystyle \int f(\sin x)\cos xdx=\int f(\sin x)d\sin x$
$\displaystyle \int f(\cos x)\sin xdx=-\int f(\cos x)d\cos x$
$\displaystyle\int f(\tan x)\sec^2 xdx=\int f(\tan x)d\tan x$

# 分子自加减

> [!example] 
> $\displaystyle \int \frac{dx}{1+e^x}=\int(1+\frac{e^x}{1+e^x})dx=x+\ln(1+e^x)+C$

> [!example] 
> $\displaystyle\int\frac{x^3}{x+1}dx=\int\frac{(x^3+1)-1}{x+1}dx=\int (x^2-x+1)dx-\int\frac{1}{x+1}dx=\frac{1}{3}x^3-\frac{1}{2}x^2+x-\ln\left|x+1\right|+C$
