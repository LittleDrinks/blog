两个重要极限：$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\sin x}{x}=1$，$\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}(1+\frac{1}{x})^x=e$
[[等价无穷小]]
化为[[一元函数积分学|定积分]]
> [!example] 
> $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(\frac{n}{1+n^2}+\frac{n}{2+n^2}+\cdots+\frac{n}{2n^2}\right)$
> $=\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{1}{n}\left(\sum_{k=1}^n\frac{1}{1+(\frac{k}{n})^2}\right)=\int_0^1\frac{dx}{1+x^2}$

换元
> [!example] 
> $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{[\sin x-\sin(\sin x)]\sin x}{x^4}$
> 令 $t=\sin x$，原式 $=\displaystyle\lim_{t\to0}\frac{t-\sin t}{t^3}=\frac{1}{6}$

[[泰勒级数|泰勒展开]]
> [!faq]- $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{e^x-1-x}{x^2}$
> $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{e^x-1-x}{x^2}=\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{1+x+\dfrac{x^2}{2!}+o(x^2)-1-x}{x^2}=\dfrac{1}{2}$
